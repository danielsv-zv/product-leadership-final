# Product Strategy One-Pager & OKRs: Meridian

> Module 1 · Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, ★ Deliverable 1
>
> Your one spine: the **Playing to Win** cascade, one deliberate **hard no**, and an **OKR cascade** that flows directly from it.
> Draft the cascade + hard no in Sprint 1, then add the OKRs in Sprint 2. The goal: specific enough that a skeptical board member couldn't poke a hole in it.

## 0. Chosen scenario

**Path:** **Meridian Foundations** (B2B · adoption + expansion)

Meridian is already bought and already audited — the gap is not the sale, it is the job site. The work only counts when the foreman uses it instead of the group text, so adoption in the field is the whole game.

## 1. Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Your choice |
|---|---|
| **Winning aspiration**: winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics | Foremen stop reaching for the group text and the phone camera, because Meridian is both easier to use and easier to do their job with. |
| **Where to play**: segment, geography, channel, use case (the no's matter too) | Top 100 US construction firms. Superintendents and foremen — the field roles, not the back office. Field capture as the use case: daily logs, photo documentation, RFI initiation. |
| **How to win**: your differentiator competitors can't easily replicate | Capture is conversational; the record is enterprise-grade. A foreman talks and photographs. Meridian turns that into a structured, compliance-ready entry in the same system finance and legal already audit. |
| **Capabilities required**: what you must be world-class at (build / buy / partner) | **Build:** a construction ontology that maps a spoken utterance to the right object — daily log vs. RFI vs. change-order precursor vs. safety observation. That mapping is the moat; everything else can be bought or partnered. |
| **Management systems**: the metrics and rituals that reinforce your choices | Weekly field activation review: % of field-role users active per project, cohorted by project and by superintendent. Cohorting by superintendent is the point — it makes adoption a named, human problem rather than an aggregate. |

## 2. Your one hard no

_One valuable thing you are explicitly choosing **not** to do, and why it protects the focus of everything above. This is a deliberate trade-off, not a backlog of deprioritized items._

> We will not take on **any new analytics, reporting, or ERP integration work for four quarters** — because that work is requested by the buyer, not the user, and every quarter we spend serving the back office is a quarter the foreman keeps using the group text. The record is only worth reporting on once it exists, and today it doesn't.

## 3. OKR cascade

_One Objective and three Key Results that flow directly from the cascade. Each KR must be a measurable **outcome**, not an output/milestone._

> **Objective:** The job site runs on Meridian, not on a group text.
>
> - **KR1:** Weekly active field-role usage across 25 pilot projects from **8% to 60%** of superintendents and foremen by **end of Q3 2027**
> - **KR2:** Share of daily logs, photo documentation and RFI initiations on those projects originating from a field device via voice or photo capture from **5% to 70%** by **end of Q3 2027**
> - **KR3:** Median time from field observation to structured record from **26 hours to under 10 minutes** by **end of Q3 2027**
