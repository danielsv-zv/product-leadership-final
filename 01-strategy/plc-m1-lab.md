# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Foremen forget group texts and phone cameras cause they prefer Meridian as it is easier to use but also easier for them to make their job
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Top 100 US construction firms, superintendents and foremen
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Capture is conversational; the record is enterprise-grade. A foreman talks and photographs. Meridian turns that into a structured, compliance-ready entry in the same system finance and legal already audit.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** Build: a construction ontology that maps a spoken utterance to the right object (daily log vs. RFI vs. change-order precursor vs. safety observation)
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Weekly field activation review: % of field-role users active per project, cohorted by project and by superintendent.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** No new analytics, reporting, or ERP integration work for four quarters.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** The job site runs on Meridian, not on a group text.
- **KR1:** Weekly active field-role usage on 25 pilot projects goes from 8% to 60% of superintendents and foremen.
- **KR2:** 70% of daily logs, photo documentation and RFI initiations on those projects originate from a field device via voice or photo capture, up from 5%.
- **KR3:** Median time from field observation to structured record drops from 26 hours to under 10 minutes.

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** You've assumed field non-adoption is a friction problem. It happens in all sectors, they think is surveillance and I clearly need evidence that the barrier is effort and not exposure.
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** What I'd change: I'd stop treating capture speed as the answer and test the exposure hypothesis before building. Two weeks, three active projects, shadowing superintendents and asking what a filed log has actually cost them. If exposure is the real barrier, the product has to make the record protective for the foreman first.
