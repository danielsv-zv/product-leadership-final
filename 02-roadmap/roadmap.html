Now 0–3 months · three bets, one question

All three share a single hypothesis and cost roughly one team-quarter combined. Each is cheap enough to have been worth running even if the hypothesis is wrong — which is why the fourth item, the evidence gate, runs alongside them rather than before them.

We bet that cutting the daily log from 14 required fields to 4 will get a complete log filed before the truck leaves the gate, for foremen closing out a shift.

The wager underneath: the form is what kills the log, not indifference. Four fields removes the abandonment point — and most of the 26-hour lag is a log postponed because it is too long to face at 4pm.

The wager underneath: the phone camera is our real competitor, and we win by absorbing it rather than replacing it. Markup makes the photo do the explaining — the fastest route to conversational capture without waiting on a dedicated app.

Not a bet — the test. We learn whether the barrier is effort or exposure.

All three bets above wager on effort. Two weeks, three active projects, shadowing superintendents and asking what a filed log has actually cost them. The M1 pressure-test produced the competing diagnosis: foremen may avoid the record because it exposes them, not because it is slow. This runs in parallel with the bets, not after them.

Hard Nos Declined for four quarters · documented so they stay declined

What a roadmap declines is the argument. Two of these were asked for by the buyer. One came from our own field research, and that is the call worth defending.

NO 1 Offline-first mobile for poor-connectivity sites Impact 4 · Effort 4 · Field research

Note that this is a high-impact item being killed — not a low score, but a bet we have not earned the right to make. It assumes the barrier is that mobile does not work well enough on site; that assumption is unvalidated, and a competing explanation is live. The asymmetry decides it: if the study says connectivity, we lose two weeks. If it says exposure, we save a quarter.

NO 2 Time-tracking module Impact 1 · Effort 5 · 3 contract negotiations

Requested in three live negotiations, which makes it a sales rock, not a product one. It is a second product with its own payroll and compliance surface — and the most surveillance-shaped item on the board. Building a stopwatch for foremen in the quarter we ask them to trust the record works directly against KR1.

NO 3 Subcontractor portal with document sharing Impact 1 · Effort 5 · Enterprise sales

It expands who we serve before we have won the users we already have. Where-to-play names superintendents and foremen; subcontractors are a different segment with a different buying motion.

The line this roadmap draws

Spend freely where a wrong assumption costs a sprint. Spend nothing where it costs a quarter of platform engineering. The three Now bets are cheap enough to run ahead of the evidence; offline-first is not. The field study is what converts it from a Hard No into a Rock, or retires it for good.
